# Team Rules: Binary Ninjas

These rules keep everyone on the same page. Every team member follows them.

---

## Rule 1: Communicate Through Text

Keep the team informed in writing (team chat or text message) about everything that affects the project.

**Always tell the team:**

- What you are implementing or starting to work on
- When you push code (what changed and which branch)
- Anything that blocks you or changes the plan
- Anything else that is important for the team to know

**Examples**

| Situation | Message to send |
|---|---|
| Starting a task | "Starting the pause menu now. I'll work in `tetris_ver2.py`." |
| Pushing code | "Pushed to `main`: added the next-piece preview and tests." |
| Hitting a problem | "Blocked on the sound code. Anyone seen this error?" |
| Changing the plan | "Moving the music feature to next sprint." |

> **Consequence:** If you don't communicate these things, you will be given less work.

---

## Rule 2: If You Claim a Task, You Own It

When you claim a task, it is yours until it is finished.

- Say in text that you are claiming it, so the team knows it's taken.
- Finish it, or tell the team early if you can't.
- Don't claim a task you don't plan to complete.
- Don't work on someone else's claimed task without asking them first.

> **Consequence:** If you don't own the tasks you claim, you will be given less work.

---

## Quick Checklist

- [ ] I told the team what I'm working on
- [ ] I told the team what I pushed
- [ ] I claimed my task in writing
- [ ] I finished the task I claimed, or told the team why I couldn't
